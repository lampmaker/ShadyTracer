ShadyTracer, inside out

Styles

The same pages, figures and code, drawn in different styles. Read in the one that suits you: the Style menu at the top right of every page switches it, and your choice is kept on this device. Each style has a light and a dark version: the Dark / Light button beside the menu switches between them, here too, so you can see every card both ways. Each card shows a small scene from the first chapters and the key to its colours, in that style.
